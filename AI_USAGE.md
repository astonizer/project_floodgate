Have used multiple AI tools for planning, generating and reviewing code including Sonnet, Luna and Gemini.

For planning the architecture and workflow, my main tool was Sonnet with Luna being the reviwer agent. Through continuous planning and correction between these two models, have come up with the plan for the service.

Most of the time went into planning the architecture and workflows, while relatively lesser time was spent on generating the code.

For cases where the tradeoffs between two different flows as devised by Sonnet + Luna were not straightforwar, Gemini model was invoked apart from small scale tasks.

Important to note, all of the tasks were monitored thoroughly after careful human review.
